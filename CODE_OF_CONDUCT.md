# Code of Conduct

RepoSync is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

Expected behavior includes constructive feedback, respect for different experience levels, and focus on the work rather than people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact method listed in [SUPPORT.md](./SUPPORT.md). Reports will be handled confidentially and fairly.
